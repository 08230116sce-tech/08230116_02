About Me

Welcome to my Portfolio! My name is Dema Lhamo. I am 20 years old and the eldest of my three siblings. I am from Samdrup Jongkhar, a bustling city in the southeastern part of the country. My father's name is Sherab Zangpo, and he is currently running a small business in Mongar. My mother's name is Tshewang Peldon, who helps with my dad's work. My hobbies are reading and photographing the sky, the moon, and sunsets. I consider myself fortunate, for in the tranquil town of Samtse, I am graced by the ethereal beauty of its sunsets, which I enjoy capturing through my lens.

Over time, I also developed a passion for website design, as it allows me to express creativity while building something meaningful with technology. Creating this portfolio has been a way to share my journey and showcase my growing skills in ICT.

In closing, from my hometown of Mongar to my academic pursuits at Samtse College of Education, I've cherished every moment. The support of my parents has been my foundation. My love for photography and the serenity of sunsets have brought me solace and inspiration. As I continue my education and life's adventures, I'll remember the simple yet profound wisdom that in the end, everything can be alright — a truth I've learned from the captivating sunsets of Samtse.

Thank you for taking this journey with me. I look forward to the path ahead, filled with new experiences and the beauty of each day's end.
